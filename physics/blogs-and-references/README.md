# 🌐 Blogs and References

## Why This Section Matters

Textbooks and courses give you the foundations — but staying connected to the physics community, reading expert commentary, and having good reference material at hand accelerates your learning enormously. These are the blogs, Q&A sites, and reference resources that working physicists and physics students actually use.

## How to Use These

- **Physics Stack Exchange** — Search here first when you're stuck on a concept. Read the highest-voted answers for deep insight.
- **Quanta Magazine** — Subscribe to the newsletter. Read one article per week to stay connected to the frontiers.
- **Sean Carroll's blog** — Follow for thoughtful commentary on foundational physics and the Many Worlds interpretation.
- **Scott Aaronson's blog** — Essential reading for quantum computing context and computational complexity perspectives.
- **HyperPhysics** — Bookmark this. Use it as a quick reference while studying any section.
- **arXiv** — Once you've completed the core sections, start browsing new papers in areas that interest you.
