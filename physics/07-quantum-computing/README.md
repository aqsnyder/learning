# Quantum Computing

## Why This Section Matters

Quantum computing harnesses quantum mechanical phenomena — superposition, entanglement, and interference — to perform computations that are intractable for classical computers. Understanding qubits, quantum gates, and algorithms like Shor's and Grover's is increasingly important as quantum hardware matures. Quantum error correction, variational quantum eigensolvers, and quantum machine learning sit at the frontier of both physics and computer science. This section bridges the quantum mechanics you've learned with practical computation.

**Prerequisites:** [Section 04 — Quantum Mechanics](../04-quantum-mechanics/README.md)

## Suggested Order

1. **3Blue1Brown — Some light quantum mechanics** — If you haven't already, build visual intuition for quantum states.
2. **Qiskit Textbook** — Interactive, hands-on introduction to quantum computing with real quantum circuits. Run code as you learn.
3. **IBM Quantum Learning** — Supplement Qiskit with IBM's structured learning paths and exercises.
4. **Shor — Algorithms for Quantum Computation** — Read the paper that launched the field. Understand why quantum computers threaten RSA.
5. **Nielsen & Chuang — Quantum Computation and Quantum Information** — The bible of quantum computing. Work through chapters on quantum circuits, algorithms, and error correction.
6. **Preskill — Lecture Notes** — Caltech's graduate-level notes for deeper theoretical understanding.
7. **Aaronson — Quantum Computing Since Democritus** — Entertaining and intellectually stimulating tour connecting complexity theory to quantum mechanics.
8. **Mermin — Quantum Computer Science** — Concise alternative textbook focused on the computer science perspective.
