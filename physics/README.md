# ⚛️ Physics — A Learning Journey

> From classical mechanics to quantum computing — the physical principles underlying simulation, modeling, and AI for science.

## Purpose

This repository is a structured curriculum for self-studying physics — from classical mechanics through quantum computing and physics-informed machine learning. It compiles the most important textbooks, lectures, papers, and online resources into a coherent learning path.

**Designed for pairing with [NotebookLM](https://notebooklm.google.com/)** — feed individual sections or papers into NotebookLM for interactive study sessions.

## Learning Path

In each section, read the 🎯 objectives, follow the core path (the 🔑 must-reads, at most three), do at least two 🛠️ exercises and answer ✅ Check Yourself from memory, then tick the section off in [PROGRESS.md](PROGRESS.md). For routes that combine curricula, see the tracks in the [Study Guide](../STUDY-GUIDE.md).
