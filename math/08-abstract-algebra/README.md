# Abstract Algebra

## Why This Section Matters

Abstract algebra studies the deep structure of symmetry through groups, rings, and fields. In physics, group theory describes the symmetries of spacetime and particle physics. In computer science, finite fields underpin cryptography and error-correcting codes. In modern ML, equivariant neural networks use group theory to build architectures that respect symmetries in data — rotational symmetry in molecular structures, translational symmetry in images, permutation symmetry in sets. Understanding algebra gives you the language to see these connections and design architectures that leverage mathematical structure.

## Suggested Order

1. **Visual Group Theory (videos)** — Start here for intuitive, visual introduction to groups and symmetry.
2. **Pinter — A Book of Abstract Algebra** — The core text for self-study. Work through groups, then rings, then fields.
3. **Judson — Abstract Algebra: Theory and Applications** — Free supplement with additional exercises and applications.
4. **Gross — Harvard lectures** — Watch for a different perspective and deeper examples.
5. **Aluffi — Algebra: Chapter 0** — Read for the modern, category-theoretic perspective when ready.
6. **Herstein or Lang** — Advanced references for deeper study.
