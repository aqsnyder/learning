# Mechanism Design

## Why This Section Matters

Mechanism design is "reverse game theory" — instead of analyzing existing games, you design the rules of the game to achieve desired outcomes. This is the theory behind auction design, voting systems, matching markets, and incentive-compatible contracts. The VCG mechanism, the revelation principle, and optimal auction theory are tools that governments and tech companies use to allocate billions of dollars worth of resources (spectrum auctions, ad auctions, kidney exchanges). Understanding mechanism design is essential for anyone working at the intersection of economics and computer science, and it's the intellectual foundation for algorithmic game theory.

## Suggested Order

1. **Börgers — Mechanism Design** — Start with this textbook for a structured introduction to the entire field.
2. **Vickrey — "Counterspeculation, Auctions"** — Read the paper that started auction theory.
3. **Myerson — "Incentive Compatibility" (1979)** — Understand the revelation principle.
4. **Myerson — "Optimal Auction Design" (1981)** — Learn how to design revenue-maximizing mechanisms.
5. **Ausubel & Milgrom — "Lovely but Lonely Vickrey Auction"** — Appreciate the gap between theory and practice.
6. **Krishna — Auction Theory** — Deep dive into auction theory for additional rigor.
7. **Milgrom — Nobel Lecture** — Watch for a masterful overview of the field's evolution and practical impact.
