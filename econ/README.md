# 📈 Economics, Decision Theory & Game Theory — A Learning Journey

> The mathematics of incentives, strategy, and coordination.

## Purpose

This repository is a structured curriculum for self-studying economics, decision theory, and game theory. It compiles the most important books, papers, video lectures, blog posts, and online courses into a coherent learning path — from basic economic thinking through frontier topics in cooperative AI and mechanism design.

**Designed for pairing with [NotebookLM](https://notebooklm.google.com/)** — feed individual sections or papers into NotebookLM for interactive study sessions.

## Learning Path

Work through the numbered sections in order. Sections 12–15 were added later and keep their numbers so nothing else is renumbered; take them at the positions shown. Each section's README contains curated resources with direct links, descriptions, and a suggested reading order within that section.

```

Section 01  →  Microeconomics
Section 02  →  Game Theory
Section 03  →  Decision Theory
Section 04  →  Mechanism Design

Section 06  →  Behavioral Economics

Section 08  →  Computational Economics
Section 09  →  Social Choice and Voting
Section 10  →  Monetary Economics and Crypto
Section 11  →  AI Alignment through an Economic Lens
Section 12  →  Economics of AI                               (take after Section 11)
Section 13  →  Empirical Economics and Causal Inference      (take after Section 01)
Section 14  →  Macroeconomics, Money and Financial Markets   (take before Section 10)
Section 15  →  Industrial Organization and Strategy          (take after Section 07)
```
