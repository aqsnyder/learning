# Essential Blogs, Newsletters, and Community Resources

Links and descriptions checked in October 2026. Blogs and newsletters are commentary: use them to find research, then read the research.

---

## Must-Follow Blogs

| Priority | Blog | Description |
|----------|------|-------------|
| 🔑 | [Marginal Revolution](https://marginalrevolution.com/) | Tyler Cowen & Alex Tabarrok — widely read economics blog, free and updated daily (as of October 2026): economics, policy, new research, culture |
| 🔑 | [Stratechery](https://stratechery.com/) | Ben Thompson — technology strategy analysis through an economics lens (aggregation theory, platforms) |
| 🔑 | [Vitalik Buterin's Blog](https://vitalik.eth.limo/) | Deep technical and economic analysis of mechanism design, governance, and crypto economics |
| | [Behavioral Scientist](https://behavioralscientist.org/) | Online magazine on behavioral science research and its applications |
| | [Overcoming Bias](https://www.overcomingbias.com/) | Robin Hanson — economics, prediction markets, signaling, futurism (now published on Substack) |
| | [The Money Illusion](https://www.themoneyillusion.com/) | Scott Sumner — monetary economics and market monetarism. Archive only: the final post was on 1 Sept 2024, and Sumner then moved to his Substack, [The Pursuit of Happiness](https://scottsumner.substack.com/) |
| | [Data Colada](https://datacolada.org/) | Uri Simonsohn, Joe Simmons & Leif Nelson — p-hacking, replication, and data forensics (their [2021 post](https://datacolada.org/98) exposed evidence of fabricated data in a famous honesty study, which was then retracted; in August–September 2026 they [reported evidence of fraud](https://datacolada.org/138) in Ariely & Wertenbroch's 2002 study of deadlines and procrastination). Essential companion to Sections 03 and 06 |
| | [Tim Roughgarden's Page](https://timroughgarden.org/) | Course materials and lecture notes on algorithmic game theory |

## Community

| Resource | Description |
|----------|-------------|
| [NBER Working Papers](https://www.nber.org/papers) | Pre-publication economics research papers |
| [SSRN Economics](https://www.ssrn.com/index.cfm/en/econ/) | Social sciences preprint server |
| [EconPapers / RePEc](https://econpapers.repec.org/) | Searchable index of about 5.5 million economics working papers, journal articles and software items (October 2026), hosted by Örebro University |
| [LessWrong](https://www.lesswrong.com/) | Community for rationality, decision theory, and AI alignment with economics overlap |
| [Alignment Forum](https://www.alignmentforum.org/) | AI alignment research — heavy overlap with mechanism design and incentives |
