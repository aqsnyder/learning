# AI Alignment, Economics, and Frontiers

## Why This Section Matters

The intersection of economics and AI alignment is one of the most important intellectual frontiers of our time. Goodhart's Law ("when a measure becomes a target, it ceases to be a good measure") is the economic framing of the reward hacking problem in AI. Mechanism design provides tools for aligning incentives between humans and AI systems. Game theory models the strategic interactions between multiple AI agents and between AI and humans. Cooperative AI research asks how to build AI systems that cooperate with humans and each other, drawing heavily on bargaining theory, social choice, and repeated games. This section connects the economic foundations from earlier sections to the cutting-edge challenges of AI governance, multi-agent coordination, and value alignment.

## Suggested Order

1. **Manheim & Garrabrant — "Goodhart's Law"** — Start with this taxonomy to understand the core economic framing of AI alignment.
2. **Amodei et al. — "Concrete Problems in AI Safety"** — Read for the broader safety context, including reward hacking.
3. **Hadfield-Menell et al. — "The Off-Switch Game"** — Study the game-theoretic analysis of corrigibility.
4. **Dafoe et al. — "Cooperative AI"** — Read the foundational paper on multi-agent cooperation.
5. **Dennis et al. — "Multi-Principal Assistance Games"** — Extend to multiple conflicting principals.
6. **Christian — The Alignment Problem** — Read for an accessible narrative that ties everything together.
7. **AI Safety Fundamentals (BlueDot)** — Take the structured course for comprehensive coverage.
8. **Alignment Forum** — Follow for ongoing frontier research at the economics-AI intersection.
