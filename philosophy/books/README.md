# Recommended Books

Do not read cover-to-cover. Use as companions to the section READMEs. Start with Blackburn then Kim then work through as needed.

| # | Priority | Title | Author(s) | Year | Free? | Level | Link | Notes |
|---|----------|-------|-----------|------|-------|-------|------|-------|
| 1 | 🔑 | Think: A Compelling Introduction to Philosophy | Simon Blackburn | 1999 | Paid | Beginner | | Excellent accessible intro to all major philosophical questions |
| 2 | 🔑 | Philosophy of Mind: A Contemporary Introduction | Jaegwon Kim | 2010 | Paid | Beginner | | Best textbook on philosophy of mind — systematic and clear |
| 3 | 🔑🔑 | Gödel, Escher, Bach: An Eternal Golden Braid | Douglas Hofstadter | 1979 | Paid | Intermediate | | Pulitzer Prize winner on minds, meaning, formal systems, and recursion |
| 4 | 🔑 | The Conscious Mind: In Search of a Fundamental Theory | David Chalmers | 1996 | Paid | Advanced | | Introduces the hard problem of consciousness. Rigorous and influential |
| 5 | 🔑 | Consciousness Explained | Daniel Dennett | 1991 | Paid | Intermediate | | Dennett's deflationary account — essential counterpoint to Chalmers |
| 6 | 🔑 | Superintelligence: Paths, Dangers, Strategies | Nick Bostrom | 2014 | Paid | Intermediate | | The foundational text on existential risk from advanced AI |
| 7 | 🔑 | Human Compatible: AI and the Problem of Control | Stuart Russell | 2019 | Paid | Beginner | | Russell's framework for beneficial AI — accessible and practical |
| 8 | 🔑 | Ethics of Artificial Intelligence | S. Matthew Liao (ed.) | 2020 | Paid | Intermediate | | Comprehensive anthology covering fairness, rights, responsibility |
| 9 | | The Embodied Mind: Cognitive Science and Human Experience | Varela, Thompson, Rosch | 1991 | Paid | Intermediate | | Foundational text on embodied and enactive cognition |

| 14 | | I Am a Strange Loop | Douglas Hofstadter | 2007 | Paid | Intermediate | | Companion to GEB — focuses on self-reference and consciousness |
| 15 | | The Mind's I: Fantasies and Reflections on Self & Soul | Hofstadter & Dennett (eds.) | 1981 | Paid | Beginner | | Anthology of mind-bending thought experiments |
