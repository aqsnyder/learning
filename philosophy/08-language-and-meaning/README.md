# Language and Meaning

## Why This Section Matters

Language is the medium through which we express thought, and large language models have forced a reckoning: if a system produces language indistinguishable from a human's, does it *mean* what it says? Wittgenstein's later philosophy demolished the idea that meaning is a private mental image, arguing instead that meaning is use within a "language game." This insight is startlingly relevant to the LLM era. This section covers the philosophy of language from Wittgenstein and Austin through Grice's conversational implicature and contemporary debates about whether LLMs understand or merely mimic.
