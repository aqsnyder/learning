# Free Will and Agency

## Why This Section Matters

If human minds are physical systems governed by natural laws, do we have free will? And if we don't, what happens to moral responsibility, praise, blame, and punishment? This question takes on new urgency in the age of AI: if we build autonomous agents, are they responsible for their actions? Can an AI be blameworthy? Dennett's compatibilism offers the most philosophically satisfying reconciliation of free will with determinism, but the debate is far from settled — Libet's neuroscience experiments, Frankfurt's thought experiments, and Sapolsky's biological determinism all challenge our intuitions about control and responsibility.
