# 🧠 Philosophy of Mind & Intelligence — A Learning Journey

> The deepest questions about minds, machines, consciousness, and what it means to understand.

## Purpose

This repository is a structured curriculum for self-studying the philosophy of mind, consciousness, and artificial intelligence. It compiles the most important books, papers, lectures, and online resources into a coherent learning path — from classical epistemology through the hard problem of consciousness to the ethics and existential risks of AI.

**Designed for pairing with [NotebookLM](https://notebooklm.google.com/)** — feed individual sections or papers into NotebookLM for interactive study sessions.
