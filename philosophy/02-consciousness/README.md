# Consciousness

## Why This Section Matters

Consciousness is the central mystery. Why does it feel like something to be you? Why aren't we philosophical zombies — beings that behave identically to us but with no inner experience? David Chalmers' formulation of the "hard problem" is one of the most important ideas in modern philosophy: explaining *why* physical processes give rise to subjective experience, not just *how* they correlate with it. This section covers the major theories (higher-order theories, global workspace, integrated information theory) and the fierce debates between those who think consciousness is a deep metaphysical puzzle and those (like Dennett) who think it's an illusion generated by our cognitive architecture. It ends with how the science is now testing rival theories head to head — and fighting over which ones count as science at all.

## 🎯 Learning Objectives

After this section you can:
- Distinguish Chalmers's "easy problems" from the hard problem and classify any given phenomenon — including LLM behaviors — by Chalmers's criteria.
- Explain Block's access/phenomenal distinction and diagnose the "target reasoning" fallacy about the function of consciousness.
- State illusionism (Dennett, Frankish) and its "illusion problem", and steelman it against a realist about phenomenal consciousness — and vice versa.
- Compare the four families of scientific theories (Seth & Bayne) by what each predicts for specific systems, and compute integrated information for toy networks.
- Design an experiment that discriminates between two theories, and explain what the Cogitate adversarial test and the IIT "pseudoscience" dispute show about testability.

## Suggested Order

**Core path (🔑):** Chalmers, *The Conscious Mind* → Dennett, *Consciousness Explained* → Seth & Bayne, "Theories of Consciousness"

1. **Chalmers — "Facing Up to the Problem of Consciousness"** — The paper that launched the modern debate
2. **SEP — Consciousness** and **IEP — The Hard Problem of Consciousness** — Map the landscape before going deeper
3. **Chalmers — The Conscious Mind** — The full, rigorous argument for the hard problem
4. **Dennett — Consciousness Explained** — The most powerful deflationary response
5. **Frankish — "Illusionism as a Theory of Consciousness"** — The deflationary view in its modern form
6. **Block — "On a Confusion about a Function of Consciousness"** — Sharpen the key distinctions
7. **Seth & Bayne — "Theories of Consciousness"** — Map the scientific theories before studying any one of them
8. **Dehaene — Consciousness and the Brain** — Global (neuronal) workspace theory
9. **Tononi — IIT** — The most mathematical (and most contested) theory
10. **Koch — The Feeling of Life Itself** — Accessible companion to IIT
11. **Lenharo (Nature) — the IIT "pseudoscience" letter** — What counts as a scientific theory of consciousness?
12. **Cogitate Consortium (2025) — adversarial test of IIT vs. GNWT** — How the two leading theories fared when tested head to head
13. **Seth — Being You** — Contemporary predictive processing account
