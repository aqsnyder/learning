# Consciousness

## Why This Section Matters

Consciousness is the central mystery. Why does it feel like something to be you? Why aren't we philosophical zombies — beings that behave identically to us but with no inner experience? David Chalmers' formulation of the "hard problem" is one of the most important ideas in modern philosophy: explaining *why* physical processes give rise to subjective experience, not just *how* they correlate with it. This section covers the major theories (higher-order theories, global workspace, integrated information theory) and the fierce debates between those who think consciousness is a deep metaphysical puzzle and those (like Dennett) who think it's an illusion generated by our cognitive architecture.

## Suggested Order

1. **Chalmers — "Facing Up to the Problem of Consciousness"** — The paper that launched the modern debate
2. **SEP — Consciousness** — Map the landscape before going deeper
3. **Chalmers — The Conscious Mind** — The full, rigorous argument for the hard problem
4. **Dennett — Consciousness Explained** — The most powerful deflationary response
5. **Block — "On a Confusion about a Function of Consciousness"** — Sharpen the key distinctions
6. **Tononi — IIT** — The leading scientific/mathematical theory
7. **Koch — The Feeling of Life Itself** — Accessible companion to IIT
8. **Seth — Being You** — Contemporary predictive processing account
