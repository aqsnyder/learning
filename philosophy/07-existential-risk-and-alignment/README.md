# Existential Risk and Alignment

## Why This Section Matters

If advanced AI systems pursue goals misaligned with human values, the consequences could be catastrophic — potentially existential. This is not science fiction: the alignment problem is a central research challenge in AI today. Bostrom's *Superintelligence* laid out the strategic landscape of existential risk from AI, while Russell's *Human Compatible* proposed a concrete technical and philosophical framework for building beneficial AI. This section covers the control problem, value alignment, instrumental convergence, and the growing research program aimed at ensuring AI systems remain safe and beneficial as they grow more powerful.
