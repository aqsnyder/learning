The frontier of ML research moves fast. This section collects the most impactful recent work across reasoning, multimodality, interpretability, agents, and ecosystem-level reports. Revisit regularly.

## Reasoning and Test-Time Compute

|---|----------|------|---------|------|

## Interpretability and Safety

| # | Resource | Type | Marking | Link |
|---|----------|------|---------|------|
| 1 | Scaling Monosemanticity – Anthropic (2024) | Paper | 🔑 | [transformer-circuits.pub](https://transformer-circuits.pub/2024/scaling-monosemanticity/) |
| 2 | Constitutional AI – Bai et al. (2022) | Paper | | [arxiv.org/abs/2212.08073](https://arxiv.org/abs/2212.08073) |
| 3 | Toy Models of Superposition – Elhage et al. (2022) | Paper | | [transformer-circuits.pub](https://transformer-circuits.pub/2022/toy_model/) |

Understanding what models learn internally is critical for safe deployment. Toy Models of Superposition explains why features are entangled, Scaling Monosemanticity extracts interpretable features from large models, and Constitutional AI shows how to bake values into training without human labels at every step.
