## Suggested Order

1. **Lopp.net Security Guide** — Survey the threat landscape and best practices.
2. **Cold storage** — Understand air-gapped signing and hardware wallet options.
3. **Multisig** — Learn multi-signature setups for stronger custody.
4. **BIP 174: PSBT** — Understand the signing workflow that makes multisig and cold storage practical.
5. **Coin selection and UTXO management** — Learn how UTXO handling impacts privacy.
6. **CoinJoin and PayJoin** — Explore collaborative transaction techniques.
7. **Chain analysis and privacy threats** — Understand how surveillance works to know what you're defending against.
8. **Bitcoin Privacy Guide** — Practical techniques to apply what you've learned.
9. **Antonopoulos on privacy** — Fill in gaps with accessible video content.
