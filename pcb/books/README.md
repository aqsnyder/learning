# Recommended Books

These books are ordered from accessible to advanced. Do not read cover-to-cover; use as companions to the section READMEs. The ⭐ starred items form Rick Hartley's recommended reading list for PCB designers.

| # | Priority | Title | Author(s) | Year | Free? | Level | Notes |
|---|----------|-------|-----------|------|-------|-------|-------|
| 1 | 🔑 | The Art of Electronics (3rd ed) | Horowitz and Hill | 2015 | Paid | Beginner | The electronics bible |
| 2 | | Practical PCB Design: From Breadboard to Production | Mike D. Smith | 2026 | Paid | Beginner | No Starch Press, October 2026, 272 pp. ([nostarch.com](https://nostarch.com/practical-PCB-design)). Takes a working breadboard circuit to a production board: component selection and tolerances, symbols and footprints, board structure, placement, routing and vias, design rules, design reviews, assembly files, ordering and testing. Examples use Altium CircuitMaker; the methods carry over to KiCad |
| 3 | | Complete PCB Design Using OrCAD | Kraig Mitzner | — | Paid | Beginner | Industry-ready DFM/DFT practices |
| 4 | 🔑⭐ | Signal and Power Integrity Simplified (3rd ed) | Eric Bogatin | 2018 | Paid | Intermediate | Most approachable SI book |
| 5 | 🔑⭐ | High-Speed Digital Design: A Handbook of Black Magic | Howard Johnson and Martin Graham | 1993 | Paid | Intermediate | The SI bible |
| 6 | 🔑⭐ | Electromagnetic Compatibility Engineering | Henry Ott | 2009 | Paid | Intermediate | Definitive EMC reference |
| 7 | ⭐ | Right the First Time Vols I and II | Lee Ritchey | 2003, 2007 | Paid | Advanced | Physics-first PCB design |
| 8 | ⭐ | Fast Circuit Boards: Energy Management | Ralph Morrison | 2018 | Paid | Advanced | How energy travels as fields (Wiley hardcover 2018; the e-book appeared in December 2017) |
| 9 | ⭐ | Principles of Power Integrity for PDN Design Simplified | Larry Smith and Eric Bogatin | 2017 | Paid | Advanced | PDN mastery |

| 11 | | Practical Electronics for Inventors | Scherz and Monk | — | Paid | Beginner | Accessible electronics reference |
