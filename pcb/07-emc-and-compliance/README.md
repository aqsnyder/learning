# EMC and Compliance

## Why This Section Matters

Your board has to work in the real world without radiating or being susceptible to interference. EMC (Electromagnetic Compatibility) is where signal integrity meets regulatory reality. Understanding emission sources, coupling mechanisms, and mitigation techniques is essential for designs that pass FCC/CE testing — and more importantly, for designs that work reliably alongside other electronics.
