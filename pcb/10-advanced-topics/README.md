# Advanced Topics

## Why This Section Matters

Taking your designs to the next level with complex routing, thermal management, and flex circuits. These topics separate competent designers from experts.

**Prerequisites:** All previous sections.

## Suggested Order

1. **BGA escape routing and via-in-pad** — Understand the concept before watching the tutorials.
2. **Robert Feranec BGA routing series** — Watch expert BGA fanout strategies in action.
3. **Thermal management** — Learn thermal vias, copper pours, and heatsink integration techniques.
4. **Open Source Thermal Simulation** — Try thermal analysis on your own designs.
5. **Phil's Lab advanced designs** — Study complete complex project walkthroughs (STM32, FPGA).
6. **Tech Explorations KiCad Advanced** — Deepen your EDA tool skills for advanced designs.
7. **RF PCB design basics** — Explore microstrip, stripline, and antenna matching fundamentals.
8. **Flex and rigid-flex PCB design** — Learn when and how to use flexible circuits.
