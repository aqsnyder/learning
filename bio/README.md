# 🧬 Biology & Computational Life Sciences — A Learning Journey

> From molecular biology to AI-driven drug discovery and protein design.

## Purpose

This repository is a structured curriculum for self-studying biology and computational life sciences. It compiles the most important textbooks, papers, video lectures, blog posts, and online courses into a coherent learning path — from foundational molecular biology through modern AI-driven approaches to understanding and engineering life.

**Designed for pairing with [NotebookLM](https://notebooklm.google.com/)** — feed individual sections or papers into NotebookLM for interactive study sessions.

## Learning Path

**Start here: [Section 00 — Molecular Biology Fundamentals](00-molecular-biology-fundamentals/).** Take the sections top to bottom: the table is in recommended order, and *When* flags the sections whose number doesn't match their place. In each section, read the 🎯 objectives, follow the core path (the 🔑 must-reads, at most three), do at least two 🛠️ exercises and answer ✅ Check Yourself from memory, then tick the section off in [PROGRESS.md](PROGRESS.md). For routes that combine curricula, see the tracks in the [Study Guide](../STUDY-GUIDE.md).

Section 12 is the one to move first: it comes before Sections 04, 05, 08 and 10, because every one of them produces numbers that need judging. Section 13 is the "how the data is made" counterpart to Section 04's "what to do with the data", and Section 14 supplies the evolutionary reasoning that alignment, conservation, constraint and GWAS all quietly depend on.
